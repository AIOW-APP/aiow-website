2. One canonical repo/project root.
3. One central status under `ops/status/`.
4. No final PASS without evidence.
5. No Book QA fakery by Handsome.
6. No stale art/direction resurrection.

1. Commander creates a task in `ops/tasks/active/`.
2. Worker executes only that task.
3. Worker writes report/evidence to assigned inbox or QA/evidence path.
4. Commander processes output.
5. Book QA's where required.
6. Commander reports to Richard.
