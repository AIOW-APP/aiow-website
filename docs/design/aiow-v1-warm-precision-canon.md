# AIOW V1 — Warm Precision Product Canon

**Selected direction:** Warm Precision v2, approved visual prototype in `TeamVault/Operations/aiow-review-inbox/aiow-prototype-v2.html`.

## Product promise

AIOW installs working AI into companies and buildings. The first useful interaction is a transparent price indication—not generic agency copy.

## Identity

- Background: `#14161A`
- Soft background: `#1B1E24`
- Card: `#20242B`
- Line: `#2E333C`
- Ink: `#F4EFE6`
- Muted ink: `#A7A297`
- Single accent: copper `#D9A441`
- Headlines: Fraunces
- UI/body: Inter
- Prices: tabular numerals

## Signature behavior

The live calculator is the hero and proof object. Switching between Business, Building and Home changes the pricing model without page navigation, preserves context and explains exactly what is excluded.

## Motion

- Meaningful count-up on price changes.
- Calm reveal/lift only.
- No decorative particle field, purple AI gradients or robot imagery.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.

## Content rules

- NL root, EN secondary.
- Direct answer in first paragraph.
- Published numbers are indications and exclude hardware/installation and customer cloud/AI usage.
- Do not invent clients, results, reviews, addresses, partnerships or rankings.
- Solutions and Ventures remain distinct propositions.
- Home/Smart Office physical installation remains partner-dependent until proven.

## Responsive/accessibility gates

- 320, 390, 768 and 1440px.
- No horizontal overflow or clipped controls.
- 44px primary interactive targets.
- Keyboard-operable calculator and modal.
- Visible focus, semantic headings, labelled fields and status messages.
- Light/dark/system controls persist without hydration mismatch.
