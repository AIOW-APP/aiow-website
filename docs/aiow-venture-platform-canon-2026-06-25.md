# AIOW Venture Platform Canon

Status: active strategic foundation
Owner: Team Richard
Date: 2026-06-25

## 1. Strategic reset

AIOW is not a website builder, chatbot vendor, automation agency or generic software shop.

AIOW is an AI Venture Platform for startups and existing companies that need a partner to build and operate the digital growth layer of the business.

AIOW can take responsibility for AI, software, automation, websites, apps, branding, SEO, GEO, marketing, data, dashboards, AI workers and digital strategy. The commercial model must match risk, value, control and upside.

Core trade:

- The entrepreneur brings market access, domain knowledge, customers, distribution, assets, proof or an existing business.
- AIOW brings AI, software, automation, growth systems, dashboards, agents, product delivery and digital execution capacity.
- The collaboration can be paid fee, retainer, revenue share, profit share, equity or a hybrid, but never vague free labor.

## 2. The current wrong direction

The current AIOW.ai direction contains useful parts, but the experience still points visitors toward the wrong mental model.

### 2.1 Website and visual surface became too important

The site tries to prove that AIOW is modern. That is not enough. AIOW must prove that it can select, assess, structure and build venture-grade digital growth opportunities.

The homepage should not feel like a premium AI agency page with a chat widget. It should feel like a selective venture entry point.

### 2.2 Chat became too central

Spunky as generic public chat makes AIOW feel smaller. A chatbox suggests chatbot vendor. AIOW needs an intake and decision system.

Spunky should be the frontdoor and context collector, not the product promise itself.

Correct role for Spunky:

- understand the visitor's opportunity
- ask one sharp follow-up question
- extract structured facts
- ask contact details only after value
- create or attach a lead/account
- prepare a Deal Card and handoff

Wrong role for Spunky:

- generic website chatbot
- long scripted answer engine
- free strategy consultant
- promise maker
- deal decider

### 2.3 Portal is too service-oriented

The current portal direction has useful pieces, but it can feel like a customer portal for quotes, planning and maintenance. AIOW needs a Deal Room first.

The first private environment should be a Deal Room with:

- opportunity profile
- founder or company profile
- proof of demand
- market and distribution context
- available data and systems
- desired AIOW role
- risk map
- Deal Card
- human decision rail
- contract or scope status
- proof sprint plan

A customer portal comes after the deal is accepted.

### 2.4 Pricing can attract the wrong people

Any public copy that sounds like low price, free build, cheap setup, hourly work or equity instead of payment is dangerous.

AIOW does not build for free. Upside is only relevant when budget, scope, control, data access, proof, reporting and contract terms are right.

Public framing:

- paid assessment first
- paid proof sprint when proof is needed
- funded build when customer wants delivery
- hybrid only when AIOW has measurable upside and enough control
- venture participation only for selected opportunities
- No-Go is a valid outcome

## 3. New product definition

AIOW Venture Platform is an operating system for moving an opportunity from idea or company problem to structured assessment, human decision, deal, project group and proof sprint.

Main flow:

1. Public positioning
2. Pre-qualification
3. Private Deal Room
4. AI assisted due diligence
5. Human Go or No-Go decision
6. Deal proposal
7. Contract or scope agreement
8. Spunky project handoff
9. Proof sprint
10. Build, scale or stop decision

## 4. Ideal customer journey

### 4.1 Public page

The public page must answer five questions fast:

1. What is AIOW?
2. Who is it for?
3. What digital responsibility can AIOW take over or build?
4. How does AIOW decide the right collaboration model?
5. How do I submit my company or idea for assessment?

Primary CTA:

Submit your company or idea for AIOW assessment.

Secondary CTA:

See how the AIOW Venture Platform works.

### 4.2 Pre-qualification

Before private data, collect enough context to filter quality:

- startup, idea or existing company
- decision maker
- current business or concept
- target customer
- proof of demand
- existing revenue or traction
- current distribution or customer access
- main bottleneck
- what AIOW should build or take over
- available data and systems
- budget or cash commitment
- openness to revenue share, profit share or equity
- urgency

### 4.3 Private Deal Room

After initial qualification, move the prospect into a private Deal Room.

The Deal Room collects:

- founder or operator context
- company and legal basics
- market and customer segments
- current revenue and commercial model when relevant
- customer proof
- data sources
- existing tools and systems
- process bottlenecks
- IP or assets
- desired AIOW role
- risks
- success metrics
- budget and upside preferences

### 4.4 AI due diligence

AI prepares an assessment. AI does not make the final decision.

Automated analysis should include:

- intake completeness
- lead quality
- founder or operator quality
- market signal
- distribution strength
- data readiness
- AI opportunity
- execution complexity
- expected value area
- risk flags
- first proof sprint candidate
- suggested collaboration model

### 4.5 Human decision

Richard, Jeroen or approved AIOW operators decide:

- No-Go
- Paid Venture Scan
- Paid Proof Sprint
- Fixed Build
- Growth Partner
- Hybrid Partner Model
- Selective Venture Deal

AI can recommend. Human decides.

### 4.6 Proposal and contract

The proposal must make these explicit:

- what AIOW builds or operates
- what the customer must provide
- required access
- scope
- budget or retainer
- upside model if any
- IP and data rules
- KPI baseline
- proof sprint criteria
- kill criteria
- decision rights
- timeline

### 4.7 Project operation

After agreement:

- project workspace is created
- Spunky receives the context package
- proof sprint tasks are created
- dashboard tracks status, risks and value receipts
- AI agents support execution
- humans approve critical decisions

## 6. Automated analyses

MVP automated analysis should be useful but honest. It is decision support, not final venture truth.

### Required analyses

1. Intake completeness score
2. Lead quality score
3. Venture fit score
4. Workflow opportunity score
5. Data readiness score
6. AI leverage score
7. Execution complexity score
8. Risk flags
9. Missing proof list
10. Recommended first proof sprint
11. Recommended collaboration model
12. Contract readiness check

### Scoring model

Use a 1 to 5 score for:

- founder or operator quality
- market quality
- distribution
- proof of demand
- data advantage
- AI opportunity
- execution complexity
- budget or cash commitment
- upside potential
- AIOW strategic fit

Human reviewers can override every score with a note.

- deal model
- revenue share percentage
- profit share percentage
- equity or participation
- contract terms
- IP ownership
- data access approval
- production launch
- external customer communication with sensitive claims
- paid ads launch

Trust message:

AI accelerates analysis. Humans make commercial, legal and strategic decisions.

## 8. Collaboration models

### Paid Venture Scan

For early ideas and uncertain companies.

Output:

- opportunity map
- risk map
- data and process audit
- first sprint recommendation
- Go or No-Go advice

### Paid Proof Sprint

For opportunities with potential but missing proof.

Output:

- one small measurable workflow or growth experiment
- baseline and result
- value receipt
- decision to stop, adjust or scale

### Fixed Build

For clients that want delivery and pay the full scope.

Output:

- scoped build
- support and optimization
- optional module or resale terms when AIOW creates reusable IP

### Growth Partner

For existing companies with budget and measurable growth potential.

Output:

- monthly operating layer
- AI, automation, marketing and dashboards
- retainer plus possible upside

### Hybrid Partner Model

Lower fee plus measurable upside. Only when reporting, decision rights and scope are clear.

### Selective Venture Deal

AIOW invests significant capacity or IP in exchange for strong upside. Only for rare, high-quality opportunities.

Requirements:

- strong founder or operator
- market access
- proof of demand
- clear data or distribution advantage
- decision rights
- legal protection
- reporting rights
- kill criteria

## 11. MVP to build first

The first real MVP is not a prettier homepage and not a full portal.

The first MVP is:

AIOW Deal Intake and Deal Card MVP.

### MVP scope

1. Public AIOW Venture Platform page
2. AI Intake Console that acts as qualification, not generic chat
3. Pre-qualification form or guided Spunky intake
4. Contact and consent capture
5. Private Deal Room account creation
6. Automated Deal Card v1
7. Admin review dashboard
8. Human decision rail
9. Contract or scope draft readiness
10. Spunky project handoff package
11. Proof sprint template

### MVP success criteria

- A real prospect can submit a company or idea
- AIOW receives structured information
- AI creates a useful Deal Card draft
- Richard or Jeroen can decide Go, Conditional Go, Adjust Deal or No-Go
- The prospect receives a clear next step
- No one expects free building
- Every step has a proof event

## 13. Immediate product changes needed on AIOW.ai

### Must change now

- homepage headline and CTA must say AI Venture Platform, not AI solution or chatbot
- chat must stop being the hero feature
- Spunky must become AI Intake Console or qualification flow
- public copy must filter low-quality leads
- pricing must enforce paid assessment and no free build
- portal language must move from customer portal to Deal Room for pre-deal prospects

### Must not build now

- full autonomous agent platform
- full revenue share accounting engine
- automatic contract negotiation
- complex marketplace
- broad SEO page sprawl as core product
- endless chat before qualification
- free strategy output before paid scan or clear fit

## 14. Operating rule from now on

Every AIOW task must answer this before implementation:

Does this strengthen the AI Venture Platform?

If the answer is no, it is not priority.

Accepted priorities:

- better opportunity intake
- better trust and selection
- better Deal Card quality
- better human decision support
- better proof sprint execution
- better value measurement
- better Spunky handoff and context retention
- better scalable personal guidance

Rejected priorities unless explicitly approved:

- generic website polish without strategic conversion impact
- generic chatbot features
- generic AI services copy
- cheap pricing display
- unqualified lead volume
- fully automated deal decisions
- broad feature build without Deal Card value

## 15. Final strategic position

AIOW should be known for one thing:

AIOW turns promising ideas and existing companies into AI-enabled ventures by taking responsibility for the digital growth layer, with a collaboration model that matches risk, control and upside.

This canon overrides older AIOW directions that frame the company primarily as AI implementation, chatbot, website, software, SEO, portal or automation provider.
