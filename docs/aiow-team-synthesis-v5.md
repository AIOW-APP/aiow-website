# AIOW Team Synthesis v5

This document consolidates the independent product, commercial and architecture reviews after Richard's Master Prompt Part 0 to Part 5.

## Three concepts

### Concept A, radical minimalism

AIOW opens as one calm first meeting. The screen contains AI presence, one message, one composer and one small memory object. This is the most Apple-like and the lowest cognitive load.

Verdict: strongest emotional trust, but may not show enough OS depth alone.

### Concept B, AI first

The AI conversation is central. Desktop uses three zones, mobile uses a standalone app shell with Chat as the larger center action. Venture Memory and Deal Card emerge progressively.

Verdict: chosen MVP direction.

### Concept C, full futuristic OS

Full suite with Home, Workspace, Chat, Team, Account, Insights, Docs, Timeline, Admin and contracts.

Verdict: strong future roadmap, too much for first public experience.

## MVP definition

The MVP is not the full OS. The MVP is the magical loop:

1. First AI meeting.
2. Live Venture Memory.
3. Opportunity Brief.
4. One sharp next question.
5. Consent and Magic Link after value.
6. Deal Card draft.
7. Human review rail.
8. Recommended route.
9. Follow-up or Deal Room.

## Immediate build decision

Step 1 and Step 2 are now implemented:

- Canon and roadmap are saved.
- Homepage uses AI Venture OS v5 marker.
- Desktop keeps three-zone AI Venture Partner layout.
