# AIOW Master Canon v5

Status: active product canon after Richard's Master Prompt Part 0 to Part 5 and desktop/mobile reference boards.

## Core position

AIOW is not a website, agency, chatbot, dashboard, SaaS platform or software vendor. AIOW is an AI Venture Operating System where the AI is the product and every interface element supports the feeling of meeting an intelligent digital venture partner.

## Non-negotiables

1. The AI is always central.
2. Data is never asked twice.
3. Every interaction must add value.
4. Every AI agent works from the same Venture Memory.
5. AI advises, humans decide partnerships, contracts, deal terms and external high-impact communication.
6. Every new function must make the experience feel simpler, not more complex.
7. Public AIOW copy must not use em dash characters.
8. Privacy is trust, not a checkbox.

## Emotional target

A first-time user should feel:

1. I trust this party.
2. This AI understands me.
3. I want this AI as my digital partner.

The user should not remember the interface. The user should remember how AIOW felt.

## Desktop UX

Desktop has three zones:

1. Left, AI Identity, status and minimal menu.
2. Center, Conversation Workspace. This receives the majority of attention.
3. Right, Live Venture Canvas. Only shows what AIOW actually knows or is actively inferring.

The desktop experience starts as a first meeting with the AI Venture Partner. The canvas and team activity emerge from the conversation.

## Mobile UX

Mobile is not responsive desktop. Mobile is a standalone app shell.

Bottom navigation:

1. Home
2. Workspace
3. Chat, larger center action
4. Team
5. Account

Home is a calm AI morning briefing. Chat is fullscreen and always reachable through the center action. Workspace uses swipeable or focused venture cards, not a dense dashboard grid.

## Venture Memory

Venture Memory starts from the first message and stores structured context:

- facts
- assumptions
- evidence
- risks
- decisions
- open questions
- contradictions
- preferences

Memory must distinguish claimed, inferred, evidenced, admin-approved and outdated information.

## Deal lifecycle

Curiosity, trust, magic link, persistent memory, complete intake, AI due diligence, internal review, proposal, digital agreement, workspace, project, long-term venture partner.

## Deal Card MVP

The first durable business object is the Deal Card. It connects intake, Venture Memory, scoring, human review, proposal readiness and follow-up.

Core Deal Card sections:

- lead identity and consent
- opportunity summary
- founder quality
- market and distribution
- commercial model
- AI due diligence
- scorecard
- risk flags
- missing proof
- human decision
- next step

## Human decision boundary

Humans decide:

- go or no-go
- deal model
- percentages
- equity or participation
- contract terms
- customer status
- high-impact external communication

AI may summarize, score, recommend, draft, prepare and follow up when consent and policy allow it.

## Design direction

Dark premium AI OS. Calm, precise, restrained. Ambient energy is AI presence, not decoration. Motion breathes every 8 to 12 seconds, never shouts. No bounce, no flashing, no gaming effects, no fake spinners.

## MVP direction

Do not build the full suite first. Prove one magical loop:

1. User opens AIOW.
2. AI starts the first meeting.
3. User types idea, business or problem.
4. AIOW builds live Venture Memory and Opportunity Brief.
5. AIOW asks one sharper next question.
6. After value, AIOW asks for name, business email and consent.
7. AIOW prepares a Deal Card for human review.
8. Admin decides next route.

## Reference board decisions

Accepted:

- desktop 3-zone layout
- mobile app-shell
- dark premium material system
- AI presence without avatar
- Venture Canvas as live intelligence
- Team transparency after activity exists
- Insights and timeline as evidence-backed offline AI proof

Rejected or delayed:

- traditional hero landing as primary experience
- dashboard metrics above intelligence
- mobile workspace grid as default
- fake active agents
- documents as generic file manager
- full platform suite before Deal Card MVP
