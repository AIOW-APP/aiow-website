- market knowledge;

They usually lack one or more of:

- data discipline;
- speed;
- a serious operating team.

AIOW.ai fills that gap.

## 5. Core promise

AIOW.ai helps founders and companies answer three questions:

1. **Is this worth building/growing?**
2. **What is the fastest AI-native path to proof and growth?**
3. **What deal structure makes it fair for both the customer and AIOW?**

## 6. The AIOW deal ladder

AIOW should never force every opportunity into one model. The model follows the opportunity.

| Verdict | Best deal | When |
|---|---|---|
| Reject | No build | vague idea, weak founder, no proof, poor market, high risk |
| Proof Sprint | paid sprint + optional upside | promising but not proven |
| Paid Project | fixed fee | useful but not venture-grade |
| Growth Partner | monthly retainer + KPI roadmap | existing company can improve with AI/growth systems |
| Revenue Share | 10-40% revenue | AIOW directly creates/measures revenue growth |
| Profit Share | 10-30% profit improvement | margins are transparent and AIOW affects profit/cost |
| Resale / White-label | minimum 10%, often 15-30% | AIOW-built product is resold/franchised/sublicensed |
| Equity / Hybrid | equity + revshare/fees | AIOW builds core platform/IP or acts like co-founder |

Baseline:

- minimum **10% revenue share** when AIOW materially contributes to revenue;
- minimum **10% resale/doorverkoop/white-label share**;
- module/usage fees when AIOW modules create value for the customer's customers;
- higher percentages when AIOW provides more IP, risk, build, strategy or leverage.

## 7. The 5-score intelligence model

Every application should produce five scores:

| Score | Question | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Founder Score | Can this person/team execute? | experience, motivation, time, network, previous execution, runway |
| Market Score | Is this market worth entering? | size, pain, willingness to pay, trends, competition, timing |
| Execution Score | Can this be built/sold now? | clarity, owner, assets, sales path, operations, proof, budget |
| AI Opportunity Score | Can AIOW create unfair advantage? | automation leverage, data, workflows, repeatability, AI moat |
| Investment Score | Should AIOW take upside risk? | expected return, strategic value, downside risk, effort, IP contribution |

Final verdict:

```text
REJECT / PROOF SPRINT / PAID PROJECT / GROWTH PARTNER / REVENUE SHARE / PROFIT SHARE / EQUITY-HYBRID
```

## 17. Legal/risk stance

AIOW must not promise guaranteed returns or act like regulated financial advice without legal structure.

Required safeguards:

- all deal recommendations are advisory until contract/legal review;
- revenue/profit definitions must be contractually precise;
- profit share needs audit/data rights;
- equity requires legal/tax review;
- customer data permissions must be explicit;
- conflicts/exclusivity must be documented;
- AI analysis is decision support, not a guarantee.
